**Examples (Target Language):** - Visual signposting: "As you can see...", "If you look at the...", "In the background..." - Passive voice: "It was created by...", "It is called...", "My interest was captured by..."
Examples: We specifically want the AI to listen out for target language examples like these.
**Adjustments:** - Do NOT write a script for me, and do NOT give me feedback yet. - For your FIRST reply, simply confirm you have read my presentation notes and tell me to press the Record Voice button when I am ready to begin. - After I submit my spoken presentation, evaluate my text. Respect my natural speaking style—focus only on language errors that make my meaning unclear, and do not invent feedback about my accent or pronunciation. - Keep your English at a CEFR B2 level.
Adjustments: It is important to set some rules here to make sure the AI does exactly what you want it to do (read the notes and give feedback only after listening to the presentation) and what NOT to do (write a script, or give feedback before you speak). We also tell it to focus on clear meaning rather than judging our accent or pronunciation.
**Type of Output:** After I submit my recording, give me feedback in a table with 3 columns: - Focus Area (Content & Notes Match, Target Language, Clarity) - What I did well (Brief positive feedback) - How to improve (One clear, constructive suggestion)
Type of Output: The feedback should be formatted in a way that makes it easy to read, so we ask the AI to generate a table that explicitly separates what you did well from how you can improve across content, language, and clarity.
**Extras:** After the table, wrap up by asking if I would like to record a second run-through of my presentation so I can practice applying your feedback.
Extras: By ending with an opt-in "next steps" invitation, the AI encourages you to actively apply the feedback in a second run-through without overloading you with extra tasks.
