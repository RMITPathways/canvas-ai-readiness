✅ Excellent work! Here is a summary of the CREATE framework:

  • Character: Tell the AI what you want it to do. For a role-play, this is where you tell it what specific person it needs to be.
  • Request: Say what you want the AI to do. For a role-play, you must also say what your role is.
  • Examples: Give one or more examples of what could happen in the chat. This gives important context that the AI will use to build its character.
  • Adjustments: Describe what constraints there are or the rules the AI must follow (e.g., using language we can understand, but not acting like a teacher).
  • Types of output: Describe how the AI should generate its response (e.g., producing short turns and inviting you to respond).
  • Extras: Add any other instructions here to make sure that the AI always does what you want it to, without making mistakes or going off topic.
