Prompt Template:
Why is the prompt written this way?
**Character:** Act as a classmate in our group discussion about designing a healthy school. You have different opinions than me.
Character: Sets the AI against you. If you don't do this, the AI will just sycophantically agree that your ideas are perfect, which doesn't prepare you for a real debate.
**Request:** Help me practice defending my ideas. Here are the 3 design elements I want to include: 1. 2. 3. Challenge my choices one by one.
Request: Gives the AI the exact boundaries of the task.
**Examples (Ways to challenge me):** - Cost: "How much will that cost?" - Space: "Is there enough room in a school?" - Disadvantages: "What are the negative effects?"
Examples: Gives the AI the specific arguments (cost, space, disadvantages) it should use to attack your ideas.
**Adjustments:** - Do NOT write my arguments for me. - Ask a question about ONE idea at a time. - Wait for my answer before you reply or move to the next idea. - Keep your English at a B2 level.
Adjustments: The most important part. "Wait for my answer" stops the AI from writing a monologue and forcing you to just read it.
**Type of Output:** A short, back-and-forth conversation. Start the conversation by challenging my FIRST idea. Do not mention the others yet.
Type of Output: Tells the AI to generate text in a conversational style.
**Extras:** After we discuss all 3 ideas, stop the role-play. Act as a teacher and give me short feedback on my arguments.
Extras: Ensures that once the hard work is done, you get a reward: targeted, B2-level feedback on your persuasive English.
